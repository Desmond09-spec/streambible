StreamBible: Free Church Livestream Lower-Thirds & Bible Presentation Software

StreamBible is a free, web-based utility designed for churches and broadcasters to display multilingual Bible verses on livestreams. It features zero-latency syncing, OBS browser source compatibility, and supports multiple translations including KJV, NIV, NLT, and more.

Key Features

  • Real-time Bible lower-thirds for church livestreams
  • Zero latency WebRTC and WebSocket synchronization
  • Compatible with OBS Studio, vMix, and Wirecast via browser source
  • Multiple Bible translations with offline caching

Use StreamBible to enhance your church's online worship experience with beautiful, cinematic scripture overlays.
